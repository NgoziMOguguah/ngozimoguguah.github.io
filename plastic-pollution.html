Research Theme

Plastic Pollution

Plastic pollution is one of the most pressing environmental challenges facing our oceans, rivers, and coastal communities. My research investigates the sources, movement, distribution, and impacts of plastic waste while developing science-based solutions that support cleaner waterways, healthier ecosystems, and more sustainable communities.

Research Overview

My research combines field investigations, spatial analysis, environmental monitoring, and stakeholder engagement to understand how plastic waste enters aquatic ecosystems, where it accumulates, and how scientific evidence can guide effective interventions and policy.

Research Focus

  • Plastic pollution monitoring
  • Pollution hotspot mapping

  • Community-based monitoring
  • Waste management solutions
  • Science communication
  • Policy development

Why Plastic Pollution Matters

Plastic waste threatens aquatic biodiversity, fisheries, tourism, public health, and coastal livelihoods. Scientific evidence is essential for understanding pollution pathways and developing practical, scalable solutions that reduce environmental impacts and strengthen sustainable resource management.

Research Objectives

My research on plastic pollution aims to understand the sources, movement, accumulation, and impacts of plastic waste in aquatic environments while developing innovative, science-based solutions that support environmental sustainability and effective policy.

Hotspot Identification

Identify and map plastic pollution hotspots across rivers, lagoons, estuaries, and coastal environments to support targeted interventions.

Pollution Pathways

Investigate how plastics move from land to water through drainage systems, urban runoff, rivers, and coastal processes.

Environmental Impact

Assess the effects of plastic waste on aquatic ecosystems, fisheries resources, biodiversity, and coastal livelihoods.

Solutions & Policy

Generate scientific evidence that informs waste management strategies, environmental policies, and community-based interventions.

Research Approach

My work combines field surveys, drone technology, GIS, remote sensing, and stakeholder engagement to monitor plastic pollution and support evidence-based environmental management.

Field Surveys

Systematic assessments of rivers, drainage channels, beaches, lagoons, and coastal environments to quantify plastic pollution.

Drone Monitoring

Using unmanned aerial systems (UAS) to capture high-resolution imagery for identifying and mapping plastic accumulation hotspots.

GIS & Spatial Analysis

Developing spatial maps that reveal pollution patterns, hotspot locations, and environmental risk areas.

Remote Sensing

Applying satellite imagery and geospatial analysis to monitor plastic pollution across large coastal landscapes.

Community Engagement

Working with local communities, government agencies, and stakeholders to improve monitoring and promote sustainable waste management.

Science Communication

Translating scientific findings into accessible reports, visualizations, documentaries, and policy recommendations.

Current Research Projects
