About Me

Marine Scientist • Fisheries Biologist • Science–Policy Leader

Biography

Dr. Ngozi Margaret Oguguah is a Chief Research Officer at the Nigerian Institute for Oceanography and Marine Research (NIOMR), Lagos, Nigeria. She is a fisheries biologist, ocean scientist, and science-policy leader with over two decades of experience in marine research, environmental sustainability, and capacity development.

Her research focuses on marine pollution, plastic pollution, fisheries biology, blue economy, climate resilience, and translating scientific evidence into effective environmental policy.

She has led multidisciplinary research projects, secured international grants, mentored early-career scientists, and collaborated with researchers and policymakers across Africa, Europe, and North America.
