Research Overview

My fisheries research investigates fish diversity, population structure, reproductive biology, habitat quality, and ecosystem interactions. Through field investigations, biological assessments, and stakeholder engagement, I generate scientific evidence that supports sustainable fisheries management, biodiversity conservation, and improved livelihoods for fishing communities.

Research Areas

  • Fish biology and ecology
  • Fish population dynamics
  • Stock assessment
  • Aquatic biodiversity
  • Sustainable fisheries management
  • Small-scale fisheries
  • Food security and livelihoods

Why Fisheries Matter

Millions of people depend on fisheries for nutrition, employment, and income. Scientific research is critical for protecting fish stocks, conserving biodiversity, strengthening coastal livelihoods, and ensuring sustainable use of aquatic resources.

Research Objectives

My fisheries research aims to generate scientific evidence that supports sustainable fisheries management, biodiversity conservation, resilient coastal communities, and food security through interdisciplinary research and stakeholder collaboration.

Fish Ecology

Investigate fish diversity, habitat preferences, reproductive biology, feeding ecology, and life-history characteristics of economically and ecologically important fish species.

Stock Assessment

Evaluate fish population structure, abundance, recruitment, mortality, and exploitation levels to support sustainable harvesting strategies.

Sustainable Fisheries

Develop science-based recommendations that balance fisheries productivity, biodiversity conservation, and community livelihoods.

Blue Economy

Support responsible fisheries that contribute to economic development, food security, and healthy aquatic ecosystems.

Research Themes

My research integrates biological, ecological, environmental, and socio-economic approaches to understand fisheries resources and promote their sustainable management.

Fish Population Dynamics

Understanding growth, mortality, recruitment, age structure, and population changes that influence fisheries sustainability.

Aquatic Biodiversity

Assessing fish diversity and ecosystem health to guide biodiversity conservation and habitat protection.

Habitat Assessment

Evaluating the condition of rivers, lagoons, estuaries, and coastal habitats that support fisheries productivity.

Small-Scale Fisheries

Studying artisanal fisheries and their contribution to livelihoods, nutrition, and local economies.

Climate Adaptation

Understanding how environmental variability and climate change influence fisheries resources and coastal communities.

Science to Policy

Providing scientific evidence that informs fisheries management plans and sustainable resource governance.
