About

A career defined by rigorous research and meaningful coastal impact.

Dr. Ngozi Oguguah brings deep expertise in fisheries biology, marine pollution science, and science-policy translation. Her work supports sustainable ocean strategies across local, national, and international platforms.

Biography

Dr. Oguguah has led interdisciplinary teams in marine research, coastal resilience planning, and environmental policy guidance. She builds partnerships that elevate evidence, community priorities, and sustainable ocean governance.

Leadership Experience

As Chief Research Officer at NIOMR, she oversees strategic initiatives that advance coastal science, stakeholder engagement, and policy-ready solutions for marine pollution and fisheries sustainability.

Research Focus

Her research spans plastic pollution mapping, fisheries assessments, blue economy pathways, and inclusive ocean governance with strong emphasis on coastal communities and science communication.

Professional Highlights

Leading partnerships across government, academia, and civil society to accelerate action for healthy oceans and sustainable coastal livelihoods.
