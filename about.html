About Me

Dr. Ngozi Margaret Oguguah

I am a Fisheries Biologist and Chief Research Officer at the Nigerian Institute for Oceanography and Marine Research (NIOMR), with a passion for advancing sustainable fisheries, understanding marine pollution, and translating scientific research into practical solutions that benefit both people and aquatic ecosystems.

Professional Biography

For more than two decades, my work has focused on understanding aquatic ecosystems and supporting sustainable environmental management through scientific research, collaboration, and public engagement. My research spans fisheries biology, marine pollution, plastic pollution, and microplastics, with a strong emphasis on generating evidence that informs conservation, environmental policy, and community action.

As Chief Research Officer at the Nigerian Institute for Oceanography and Marine Research (NIOMR), I lead and contribute to multidisciplinary research projects that address pressing environmental challenges affecting Nigeria's inland and coastal waters. My work combines field investigations, laboratory research, environmental monitoring, and stakeholder engagement to develop practical, science-based solutions for healthier aquatic ecosystems.

Beyond research, I actively mentor early-career scientists, contribute to international scientific collaborations, and communicate research through publications, conferences, workshops, documentaries, and public outreach. I believe that excellent science achieves its greatest impact when it is accessible, collaborative, and translated into actions that improve environmental sustainability and human well-being.

Professional Career

Research Interests

My research explores the interactions between aquatic ecosystems, environmental change, and human activities, with the goal of supporting sustainable fisheries and healthier marine environments.

Fisheries Biology

Fish ecology, biodiversity, stock assessment, sustainable fisheries, and aquatic resource management.

Marine Pollution

Water quality assessment, contaminant monitoring, ecosystem health, and pollution management.

Plastic Pollution

Plastic pollution monitoring, hotspot mapping, community engagement, and environmental solutions.

Microplastics

Occurrence, distribution, laboratory analysis, ecological impacts, and environmental risk assessment.

Aquatic Ecosystems

Understanding ecosystem dynamics to support conservation, resilience, and sustainable resource use.

Science Communication

Making research accessible through publications, presentations, documentaries, outreach, and public engagement.
