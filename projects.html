Major Project Highlights

Each project addresses real-world environmental challenges by combining scientific research, collaboration, and practical solutions that benefit aquatic ecosystems and coastal communities.

Plastic Pollution Hotspot Mapping

This project investigates the distribution and movement of plastic waste within rivers, drainage channels, lagoons, and coastal waters. By identifying pollution hotspots, the research provides evidence that supports targeted clean-up efforts, improved waste management, and environmental policy development.

  • Field surveys
  • Plastic hotspot identification
  • Environmental risk assessment
  • Stakeholder engagement

Plastic Peril Documentary

An outreach initiative translating scientific research into compelling visual storytelling that raises awareness about plastic pollution, promotes public understanding, and supports environmental education.

  • Science communication
  • Public awareness
  • Environmental education
  • Community engagement

Marine Pollution Monitoring

Long-term monitoring of aquatic ecosystems to evaluate water quality, contaminants, heavy metals, and ecological health. Findings contribute to improved environmental management and conservation planning.

  • Water quality assessment
  • Heavy metal analysis
  • Ecosystem monitoring
  • Environmental reporting

Fisheries Biology Research
