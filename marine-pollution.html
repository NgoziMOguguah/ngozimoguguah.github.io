Marine Pollution

Healthy oceans are essential for biodiversity, fisheries, food security, and sustainable development. My research investigates marine pollution, water quality, ecosystem health, and environmental contaminants to generate evidence that supports conservation, sustainable management, and informed policy decisions.

Research Overview

Marine pollution threatens aquatic biodiversity, fisheries resources, ecosystem services, and human well-being. My research examines physical, chemical, and biological indicators of pollution within coastal and inland aquatic ecosystems, with particular emphasis on Lagos Lagoon and other Nigerian waters.

Research Areas

  • Water quality assessment
  • Marine pollution monitoring
  • Heavy metal contamination
  • Coastal ecosystem health
  • Environmental risk assessment
  • Pollution source identification
  • Sustainable environmental management

Research Significance

Understanding pollution patterns and ecosystem responses provides the scientific foundation for environmental management, pollution control, fisheries conservation, and sustainable use of marine resources.

Research Objectives

My marine pollution research aims to understand the sources, distribution, impacts, and management of contaminants affecting coastal and inland aquatic ecosystems while providing scientific evidence for sustainable environmental management.

Water Quality Monitoring

Assess the physical, chemical, and biological characteristics of aquatic ecosystems to determine environmental health and identify pollution trends.

Heavy Metal Assessment

Evaluate the occurrence, distribution, and ecological risks of heavy metals in water, sediments, and aquatic organisms.

Environmental Risk Assessment

Identify pollution sources and evaluate their impacts on biodiversity, fisheries, ecosystem services, and human health.

Science-Based Management

Provide evidence that supports environmental regulations, pollution control strategies, and sustainable marine resource management.

Monitoring Framework

Marine pollution assessment combines field investigations, laboratory analyses, spatial mapping, and environmental modelling to understand ecosystem condition and pollution dynamics.

Field Sampling

Routine collection of water, sediment, and biological samples from rivers, estuaries, lagoons, and coastal waters.

Water Quality Analysis

Measurement of temperature, pH, dissolved oxygen, conductivity, salinity, turbidity, nutrients, and other environmental indicators.

Laboratory Analysis

Determination of heavy metals, contaminants, and physicochemical properties using standard analytical methods.

GIS & Spatial Analysis

Development of pollution distribution maps and environmental risk assessments to support management decisions.

Statistical Analysis

Application of statistical tools to identify pollution trends, seasonal variations, and relationships among environmental variables.

Policy Translation

Transforming scientific findings into practical recommendations for environmental agencies, policymakers, and coastal managers.
